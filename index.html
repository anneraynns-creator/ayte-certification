Three Steps to Your Certificate
01
Register & Select
Enter your name and email, then choose the Work Readiness competency you have completed and want to be assessed on.
02
Take the Assessment
Answer 10 multiple choice questions based on the AYTE curriculum. You have up to 3 attempts. Score 70% or above to earn your certificate.
03
Get Your Certificate
Pass to receive a verified certificate with a unique ID — instantly downloadable and logged in the AYTE database to track your career progress.
